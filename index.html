What is Mehfil-E-Baithak?

Mehfil-E-Baithak began as simple, organic musical sittings among close friends in a small balcony in Kalyan. Tired of the loud, commercial, and detached clubbing setups, we craved an evening where words had depth and melodies had silence to breathe.

What started as intimate sittings has blossomed into a beautiful cultural movement. Today, it stands as a community where strangers become immediate friends, and beautiful acoustic sittings turn into memories that stay forever.

We create soulful sittings through live ghazals, shayari, bansuri chords, unplugged guitar plucks, and storytelling. This is not just a show. It is a feeling.

अटूट सफ़र • OUR EVOLUTION • प्रवास सुरांचा

How the Melody Matured

Tracing our steps from local balcony sittings in Kalyan to premium candlelight theater sittings.

2023 · THE CHORDS

Cozy Balcony

सुरीली बालकनी • मैत्र आणि सूर

8 friends. 1 guitar. Unfiltered emotions. It began on an autumn evening with eight friends, an old acoustic guitar, and pure acoustic chords in the background. We spoke in couplets and sang till midnight.

2024

Expanding Circle

दायरा बढ़ता गया • भावनांचा प्रवास

Where strangers became companions. Word of mouth carried the aroma of our evenings. We moved to a cozy candle-lit terrace in Kalyan. Strangers began to join, bringing their own stories and shayaris.

2025

First Public Baithak

पहली सार्वजनिक बैठक • स्वर-मंच

A beautiful validation of pure feeling. We officially launched Mehfil-E-Baithak as a public movement. In partnership with intimate spaces, we hosted our first ticketed gathering. It sold out in just under four hours.

2026

Intimate Auditoriums

अंतरंग सभागृह • मनाचे सूर

A growing community bound by poetry and strings. Today, we curate soulful candle-lit musical sittings across premium venues in Kalyan and Dombivli. While the spaces are larger, the heart remains as intimate as that first balcony.
